How It Works
Simple. Fast. Automated.
1
Vendor lists surplus
At end of day — photo, quantity, pickup window. AI auto-sets optimal price between breakeven floor and market rate. Under 30 seconds.
2
Customers browse rescue deals
Native "Rescue Food" tab in the Snoonu app. Filter by category, distance, cuisine. Live countdown timers drive urgency.
3
Reserve and pay in-app
One tap reservation, prepaid via Snoonu wallet. Unique QR pickup code generated instantly. No cash, no friction.
4
Pickup or delivery — everyone wins
Walk in and scan code, or get it delivered via Snoonu's existing fleet. Vendor earns. Customer saves. Waste eliminated.
